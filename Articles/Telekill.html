TELE//KILL


I am a fond of one-hit-kill games, namely Katana ZERO and Hotline Miami. So I wanted to make something like those with my own twists. Telekill is a one-hit-kill 2D action platformer game with puzzle-like levels and 5 chapters each introducing different mechanics from basic guns to katanas to lasers.
Warning: Currently Chapter 1 is done. Chapter 2 only misses some tweaks and Chapter 3 is in development. The build on itch.io is old. It has only Chapter 1 and has many bugs. I'll update it sometime in 2026.
